# Hawk Academe website: admin guide

This guide is for the Hawk Academe staff who look after the website. You do not need any technical knowledge.

## Logging in

1. Go to **yourwebsite/admin/login**. The **Admin Login** button is also at the top of every page.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. The first time, you will be asked to choose your own password. It must be at least 10 characters long. A short sentence, such as `Our toppers 2026!`, is easy to remember and hard to guess.

Good to know:
- After 2 hours with no activity you are logged out, to keep the site safe.
- After 5 wrong passwords your account is locked for 15 minutes.
- If you forget your password, ask the website developer to reset it.

## Overview

This is the first screen after you log in. It shows new enquiries, posts, notices and photos, and has quick buttons for the most common jobs.

## Enquiries (Contact form)

Every message sent from the Contact page appears here, and is also emailed to the office address.

1. Open **Enquiries**. New messages appear first, with a red count in the menu.
2. Click **View** to read the whole message.
3. Call or WhatsApp the parent.
4. Click **Mark handled**. Handled enquiries move to the **Handled** tab, and the name of the person who handled each one is recorded.
5. **Export CSV** downloads the list. It opens in Excel or Google Sheets.

## Notices / News

Notices are things like new batch dates, test dates and results. They appear on the News page, and the latest four appear on the Home page.

1. Click **+ New notice**.
2. Fill in the title, the details, the type (Batches, Results or Notices) and the date.
3. If you like, add a link to a page on the site, such as `/programs`.
4. Tick **Pin to the top** to keep the notice first.
5. Untick **Show on the website** to hide it without deleting it.

## Blog

1. Click **+ New post**.
2. Fill in the title, the category and a short summary. The summary is shown on the blog list and in Google.
3. **Cover**: upload a photo. If you have no photo, pick one of the coloured subject bands.
4. Write the article. Use the toolbar for:
   - **H2** and **H3**: headings
   - **B**, **I** and **U**: bold, italic and underline
   - **Link**: add a link
   - **List**: bullet or numbered lists
   - **Quote**: a highlighted quote
   - **Picture**: a picture inside the article
5. Choose one of these:
   - **Publish** puts the post live straight away.
   - **Save draft** keeps the post hidden from visitors.
   - To **schedule** a post, pick a future date and time under *Publishing*. The post appears on its own at that time.
6. To take a post down, use **Unpublish** or **Delete** in the blog list.

## Gallery

1. Drag photos onto the box, or click **Choose photos**. You can add up to 20 at a time, each up to 10 MB.
2. Pick a category, and add a caption and a short description.
3. Click **Upload**. Photos are made smaller automatically, so the site stays fast.
4. Use the **←** and **→** arrows to change the order. **Edit** changes the caption, and **Remove** deletes the photo.

## Timings & highlights

On this screen you can change:
- the office hours
- the usual reply time
- the batch details for each programme (classes, duration, batch size, mode and timings)
- the four highlight numbers on the Home page
- the phone, WhatsApp, email and address
- the Google Map
- the social media links

When you are done, click **Save all changes** at the bottom.

## Reviews

Add testimonials from students and parents. The first six appear on the Home page. Use the arrows to change their order.

Only publish a review with the person's permission. For a student under 18, get a parent's permission too.

## Account

Change your password or your name here.

There are two admin logins, one per person. Please do not share yours.
